# Getting started

Install ASR Pro, pick a microphone, choose a local Whisper model, and record a short clip. Speech, transcripts, settings, and history stay local unless you move or export them.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Download a release for your platform. |
| 2 | Launch ASR Pro and let it create local data folders. |
| 3 | Pick the microphone in Sound. |
| 4 | Choose a Whisper model in Models library. |
| 5 | Record a short clip from Home, the tray, or the shortcut. |
| 6 | Use History to search, copy, reopen, delete, or reprocess clips. |

## Keep it working

| Case | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Moving the app | Move the executable or app bundle together with `asrpro-data/`. |
| Open on sign-in | Turn on `Launch at startup` from Configuration. If the app moves later, toggle it off and on again. |
| Wrong microphone | Refresh devices from Sound and pick the intended input again. |
